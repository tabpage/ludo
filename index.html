    How to Play

    Be first to bring all four of your tokens to the centre.

    1. Roll a 6 to leave the yard. A token needs a 6 to enter the board onto your coloured start cell.
    2. Race clockwise. Move one token by the dice value. After a full lap you turn into your own coloured home column.
    3. Exact roll to finish. The centre must be reached exactly — a roll that overshoots cannot be used by that token.
    4. Capture. Landing on a cell holding opponent tokens sends them back to their yard. Safe cells (stars and coloured start cells) never allow captures, and home columns are always safe.
    5. Bonus rolls. Rolling a 6, capturing, or bringing a token home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits your turn.
    6. No legal move? Your turn passes automatically. Your own tokens may share a cell; there are no blockades.
    7. Controls. Tap or click the dice (or press Space / R) to roll, then tap a glowing token — or press 1–4. P pauses.
